Mani

Turn your future into a board you can look at every day

Mani is a private vision board for your goals, images and the words that give them meaning. Capture what matters, revisit it daily, and keep it a glance away on your Home Screen.

Vision board

Collect the images that represent where you're headed. Add a title, a life area and your own words on why each one matters.

Goals & notes

Break visions into concrete goals and everyday notes, with an optional daily reminder to keep momentum.

Mani+ story

Turn your board into a short, clearly labelled future story — an imagined narrative built from your own words, never a prediction.

Home Screen widget

Keep your board within view with an iOS widget that shows your visions right on your Home Screen.

Private by design

Your board lives in your own private account, protected by per-account access rules. An explicit local preview mode lets you try Mani without syncing anything to the cloud — it never silently replaces a cloud connection.
