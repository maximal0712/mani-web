Common questions

How do I switch between local preview and my cloud account?

Local preview is an explicit, separate mode for trying Mani without an account. You can switch modes from Settings inside the app; it never happens automatically and never substitutes for a failed cloud connection.

How do I manage or cancel Mani+?

Mani+ subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel anytime on your iPhone under Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or see Apple's subscription help. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not delete your Mani account or content.

How do I delete my data or account?

Contact us at the address above and we'll delete your account and associated data. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we store and how long we keep it.

Something isn't working — what should I include?

Please include your device model, iOS version, the app version, and a short description of what you expected to happen so we can help faster.
